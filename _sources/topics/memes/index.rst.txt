Gravestones
-----------
Inscriptions on Gravestones should not be funny. That's simple wrong, but sometimes they are and usually, that happens 
by accident or chance.

.. thumbnail:: ../../media/Memes/gravestone.jpg
  :align: center
  :width: 60%
  :title: Interesting inscription on this gravestone. <b>Rammelt</b> is a common last name in German speaking 
     regions of Europe. It is also a slang term that means <b>„to fuck“</b> and Alfred and Gertrud are two male and female
     given names. So essentially, the inscription means <b>„Alfred fucks Gertrud“</b> 😃
  :show_caption: true
  :class: align-center-full bordered exborder
  :group: general

.. rst-class:: vpad

.. raw:: html

  The next is another example. <b>„Die Mutter war's“</b> is the beginning of a common German phrase, which reads in full:

|clear|

  „Die Mutter war’s. Was braucht’s der Worte mehr.“

Literally, this means *„A Mother she was, no more words are needed“* and it expresses love and admiration for a person 
who was a loving Mother and that single word **Mother** shall be enough to describe her.

However, since the second part of the sentence is missing (for reasons unknown) the phrase could also have a completely 
different meaning. Literally translated, *„Die Mutter war's“* simply means *„The mother did it“* and could mean, that 
poor Julie was murdered by her Mother. Which is very likely not the case here and poor speculation based on an (maybe 
deliberately) incomplete grave inscription, assuming that the fellow reader is educated enough to know the full 
phrase and its meaning.

Funny? Not really. Interesting? Certainly.
